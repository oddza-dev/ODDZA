3. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up or log in we collect your email address and your name. You can sign in with email and password or with your Google account. Passwords are handled by a secure third-party authentication service — they are stored in protected form and are never visible to us. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email address from Google.

Usage information. For signed-in users we keep a simple activity record: an anonymous visitor ID generated on your device, the date you first and last visited, how many visits you have made and how many days you were active. We use this to understand how the Service is used and to keep it reliable.

Premium access information. When you redeem an access code we record which premium category was unlocked, when it was unlocked and when it expires, so your access works correctly and can be restored when you log in again.

Support chat. If you chat with our team, we store your messages and any images you send, together with your name and email, so we can reply and resolve your request.

Push notification data. If you allow notifications, our notification provider assigns your device a notification token so we can send you alerts about new content. You can turn notifications off at any time from the notification panel in the app or in your device/browser settings.

Information stored on your device. The Service uses your browser's local storage to remember things like your language choice, your sign-in session, notification prompt preferences and your visitor ID. We do not use local storage for advertising.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store payment card or mobile-money details inside the app. The app does not process payments; any payment for premium access is arranged separately and handled by the payment provider you choose.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files — except images you choose to send in the support chat.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To unlock and manage your premium access.
  • To deliver content, history and results.
  • To send notifications about new content and important service messages.
  • To answer your messages and provide support.
  • To measure usage, fix problems, and prevent abuse, fraud and code sharing.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

6. Why we are allowed to use your information

  • To provide the Service you asked for — running your account, delivering content and unlocking premium access.
  • Your consent — for example, push notifications, which you can switch off at any time.
  • Our legitimate interests — keeping the Service secure, preventing abuse, fraud and code sharing, and improving reliability.
  • Legal obligations — when the law requires us to keep or share certain information.

7. Service providers we use

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate the Service. They process data only to provide their services to us, and include:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers — account authentication, data storage and file storage.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in with your Google account.
  • Push notification provider — delivery of notifications you have allowed.
  • Font delivery services — fonts used to display the app.

These providers have their own privacy policies. Some of them store or process data on servers outside Tanzania and outside your country. When this happens, we only use providers that apply recognised security safeguards to protect your information.

8. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the Service.

9. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Support chats and premium access records may be kept for a reasonable period afterwards for support, security and record-keeping. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except what we must keep to meet legal obligations or to prevent fraud and abuse.

10. Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secured cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong password and keep your access codes private.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see your name and email in My Account and ask us to correct them.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing info.oddza@gmail.com from the address registered on your account. We act on verified requests within 30 days.
  • Copy of your data: you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw any consent you gave (for example for notifications) at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in the app or your device settings.
  • Local data: you can clear your browser's site data, which will sign you out.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under data protection law, including under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 — such as the right to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise them. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Users must confirm they are 18 or older to use the Service. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Third-party links

The Service may link to other websites or apps. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make significant changes we will notify you in the app. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
